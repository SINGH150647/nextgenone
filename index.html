About NG One

NG One is a small product studio focused on one problem: most learning apps are built for grown-ups to approve, not for students to enjoy. We design differently — starting from what keeps a student opening the app on day 30, not just day one.

Climb Up is our first product: a class-wise learning path for students from Class 2 to Class 12, built around steady progress, daily streaks, and rewards that feel worth earning.

Built for students, not just parents

Every screen is designed to be opened by a 10-year-old without help — simple, visual, and quick to understand.

Content that keeps up with the classroom

Lessons, chapters and quizzes are managed centrally, so what a student sees stays current without needing a new app update.

Progress you can actually see

Streaks, CP and a visible learning path turn daily practice into something students want to keep up, not something they're told to do.
